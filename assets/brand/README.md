# Cutroom 品牌素材

一段胶片向前翻折，围出 Cutroom 的首字母 C。浅蓝背面沿弧线转入暖白正面，正面露出两格画幅和一排齿孔，右上方的黄绿色短片与胶片斜切口相呼应。工作台使用蓝底方标，单色符号保留镂空画幅和齿孔。

| [单色符号](cutroom-symbol.svg)                                                      | 可随背景切换颜色的矢量图形                  |
| [Cutroom 字标](cutroom-wordmark.svg)                                                | 已转为轮廓的透明矢量字标                    |
| [macOS 图标源文件](cutroom-app.svg) · [1024 像素 PNG](../../desktop/build/icon.png) | 应用包、Dock 和听写组件                     |

| [安装窗口背景](../../desktop/build/background.tiff)                                 | Finder 1× 和 2× 图像，箭头指向 Applications |

主色为钴蓝 `#2345E8`，胶片背面为浅蓝 `#A7BBFA`，正面为暖白 `#F9F8F1`，剪接片为黄绿色 `#DAF65C`。字标来自仓库内 Noto Sans SC 字体的 720 字重，沿用其 [OFL 许可证](../../desktop/tests/visual/fonts/noto-sans-sc/LICENSE)。矢量标记为本项目绘制。

## 封面制作

胶片摄影风格底图由内置 ImageGen 生成。封面排版使用项目的矢量符号和字标，中文排版单独合成。下方为底图的完整提示词。

> Use case: logo-brand. An extremely wide, premium editorial cover photograph for Cutroom, an independent macOS film editing application. Landscape 2.5:1 horizontal banner. A matte electric cobalt-blue cutting-room tabletop viewed from overhead. Confine a single art-directed physical sculpture of two bold ivory 16mm film leader strips and a tiny fluorescent chartreuse splicing tab to the RIGHT half. The top ivory leader has a fresh diagonal cut, the second piece feels suspended just above, and the loose strip curls with a soft dramatic shadow; a few subtle sprocket holes punctuate just one edge. Cropped large, tactile molded paper or translucent acetate, directional afternoon studio light, analog film grain. The left 46% is completely quiet uninterrupted rich cobalt negative space for the separate brand lockup, full bleed. Excellent modern Swiss cinema-catalogue graphic design, expressive but spare. Absolutely no text, lettering, logos, watermarks, UI, picture frames, scissors or clapperboard. Colors cobalt #2345E8, warm white and a small chartreuse accent. Do not place a border or mock up an interface.
